  - title: Live Amplification
    paragraphs:
      - Mic Facing turns your iPhone into a portable voice studio that shapes your voice in real time.
      - Pick from 12 built-in presets — Clean Voice, Hearing Assist, Podcast, Lecture Hall, and more — or save your own custom presets.
      - A half-circle gain slider with haptic detents at 2.5×, 5×, 7.5×, and 10× lets you dial in the exact level by feel.
      - Built-in EQ, compressor, and noise gate give you studio-grade control without the studio.
      - Smart room detection listens to your environment and suggests a preset that fits — Quiet, Normal, Noisy, or Loud.

  - title: Recording with iCloud Sync
    paragraphs:
      - Tap Record and capture a clean take. Audio is captured independently of the live monitor, so route changes and interruptions never truncate the file.
      - Recordings sync to iCloud Drive automatically, so they're available on every device.
      - Files are stored as plain audio in regular folders — not a proprietary database — so your data stays portable.
      - Multiple recording formats supported, including M4A, WAV, AIFF, and CAF.
      - Trim recordings to clips, organize them into folders, and add per-recording notes for context.
    image: /images/apps/micfacing/AllFiles.png
  - title: Transcription & AI Summaries
    paragraphs:
      - Auto-transcribe runs after each recording (toggle in settings) using Apple's on-device Speech framework.
      - Twelve languages supported, including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi, and Russian.
      - Tap a word in the transcript to seek to that moment in the recording.
      - AI summaries are generated by Apple's on-device Foundation Models — they never leave your iPhone.
      - Full-text search across notes, transcripts, summaries, and translations.
    image: /images/apps/micfacing/FileTranslationSummarized.png
  - title: Live Translation
    paragraphs:
      - Speak into the mic and see your words translated in real time using Apple's on-device Translation framework.
      - Twelve languages supported, with translation running fully offline once the language models are downloaded once via iOS Settings.
      - No translation servers, no accounts — just your iPhone.
    image: /images/apps/micfacing/Translate.png
  - title: Conversation Mode
    paragraphs:
      - Two people, two languages, one screen.
      - Conversation mode flips half of the screen so you and someone facing you can each tap to talk and read each other's translation in their own language.
      - Switch between any of the twelve supported languages on the fly.
      - Like everything else in Mic Facing, conversation mode runs entirely on-device.
    image: /images/apps/micfacing/LiveTranslation.png
  - title: External Mic Support
    paragraphs:
      - Plug in a USB-C mic (such as a Rode or FiFine) and Mic Facing routes input through it automatically.
      - The whole UI tints green for clear visual confirmation that your external mic is in charge.
      - Plug-and-play — no setup or pairing required.
      - Audio import also supported from Files, AirDrop, or directly from external storage.
    image: /images/apps/micfacing/ExternalMic.png
  - title: Privacy Policy
    paragraphs:
      - Mic Facing and its developer take your privacy very seriously. There are no accounts, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and no servers — Mic Facing has no backend, so there is nowhere for your data to be sent.
      - Microphone audio is captured locally on your iPhone for live amplification and recording. It is never transmitted off the device by Mic Facing.
      - Speech recognition uses Apple's Speech framework with on-device transcription where supported, so audio does not leave your device. The resulting transcript is saved to the recording's folder on your device (and to iCloud Drive, if enabled).
      - Translation uses Apple's Translation framework and is performed on-device using language models you may need to download once via iOS Settings. Mic Facing has no access to translation servers.
      - AI summaries use Apple's on-device Foundation Models. The transcript is processed locally on your iPhone and nothing is sent to a remote AI service.
      - If you have iCloud Drive enabled in iOS Settings, recordings are stored in your private app container in iCloud Drive. iOS itself handles sync between your devices — Mic Facing has no servers in this loop and no access to the contents of your iCloud Drive.
      - Permission for Mic Facing to access the microphone or speech recognition can be revoked at any time in the iOS Settings app (Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone / Speech Recognition).
